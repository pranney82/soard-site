# Live broadcast system

How live room reveals reach the site, Facebook and YouTube with nobody pressing anything on reveal day. Written for whoever maintains sunshineonaranneyday.com.

## The shape of it

```
phone (Larix, SRT) ──▶ Cloudflare Stream live input ──▶ simulcast ──▶ Facebook page (persistent key or Live Video API)
                              │                                    └──▶ YouTube (stream key, auto-start)
                              ├──▶ recording (automatic) ──▶ replay on the live page, captions, kid page link
                              ├──▶ Stream player on /live/<kid>/  (low-latency HLS)
                              └──▶ lifecycle endpoint ◀── site polls ──┐
                                                                      │
Cloudflare Notification (connect/disconnect) ──▶ /api/stream-webhook ─┤
soard-live-hub Durable Object (alarm every 8–60 s) ──▶ /api/broadcast-hooks ─┤
                                                                      ▼
                                          functions/api/_broadcast.js state machine (D1)
                                                                      │
                     ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┐
                     ▼                                                ▼                          ▼
        WebSocket push to every open page              reminders (Resend / Web Push)   alerts (Slack / email / staff texts)
        (LIVE pill, live page, admin)                  Kit drafts, audit log, analytics rows
```

**Kit "we're live" email.** Checked every tick while live (`kitAutoLive` in `_broadcast.js`). It goes out once two things are true: the stream has stayed up 3 unbroken minutes (`state.segmentAt` resets on every reconnect, so a quick phone test emails no one), and the kid is known. The kid comes from, in order: a schedule, the admin's "Which kid is this?" picker (or anything else that sets `state.kidSlug`, such as a waiting screen), the Facebook live post's title/description, then the Page's own posts from the last day, newest first (`fbRevealClues` + the archiver's `matchKids`, open kids only: no reveal video yet, this year or last; a clue naming two kids is skipped). `state.kidVia` records which. Kit sends it about a minute later (`send_at`) to `broadcast-config.kitLive.audience` (default: all subscribers), once per broadcast (`broadcast_sends` kind `live`, channel `kit`). Held with an admin alert when: a scheduled reveal starts more than 30 minutes early, no kid turns up within 30 minutes, or the kid is found by Facebook clues after 30 minutes. The emergency manual banner never sends by itself. "Email the list now" in Admin → Broadcast → Now covers every held case and retries a failed send. Each reveal also has an on/off switch (`state.kitLiveEmail`).

Stream is the origin because it gives us an authoritative "is video arriving" signal, an instant permanent recording, simulcast without a third-party service, and a player we control. Facebook stays the social destination.

## Phases

| Phase | Meaning | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| `idle` | nothing planned, no banner | expiry, cancel, reset |
| `scheduled` | a reveal has a date; countdown + reminder signup on site | JobTread's next confirmed reveal (`_broadcast-schedule.js`) |
| `armed` | within 30 min of the date; hub polls every 8 s | timer |
| `live` | Stream lifecycle says the input is receiving video | reconcile |
| `ending` | input dropped; 45 s grace before ending | reconcile |
| `replay` | broadcast over; "Watch the replay" for `replayHours` (default 72) | reconcile / admin |

An unplanned stream (phone connects with nothing scheduled) creates a broadcast on the fly with the default title. A scheduled reveal that never streams clears itself 4 h after its time and alerts.

**Scheduling comes from JobTread** (`functions/api/_broadcast-schedule.js`). The next open job with Reveal Date Confirmed = Yes, a Reveal Date with a time, and a job name matching a published kid ("Max Sample (2026)") becomes the scheduled broadcast, through `adminAction` as user "JobTread", so reminders, Kit drafts, History and the audit log work as before. `state.jobId` (and `broadcasts.data.jobId`) record the job. The check runs right away when a JobTread workflow posts to `/api/reveal-sync` or `/api/reveal-invites`, every 5 minutes on the hub heartbeat (`broadcast-hooks.js`), on `/api/reveals` traffic, and when the admin loads (at most once a minute) or presses Check now (`syncSchedule`). A D1 lock row (`site_config` "broadcast-schedule-lock") keeps two wake-ups from planning the same reveal twice. Rules: a new time on the same day moves the broadcast in place; a new day also clears the `moved`/`day`/`hour` sends so they re-arm, sends a "New date" reminder when more than 26 h out, and makes a fresh Kit draft; another reveal becoming sooner replaces it; unconfirming or clearing the time cancels it. Live and ending are never touched. Past its start time, only a new time for the same job moves it. A replay hands over to the next reveal in its last 24 h, once the recording is saved and the Reel is finished (or 6 h after it ended). A stream that went live up to 12 h before a reveal's time counts as that reveal. Confirmed reveals that can't be planned (no time, no matching kid, kid page a draft) are listed in `site_config` "broadcast-schedule" and shown on Reveals → Now. When no kid matches the job's year but exactly one does under the year before or after (jobs carry the application year, kid pages the reveal year, e.g. "Adrian Scala (2025)" vs Adrian 2026), that kid is named as a suggestion only; it is never used. Staff get a Slack/alert-email message when the countdown goes up, moves or comes down, and once per problem for a confirmed reveal in the next 21 days that can't have one (`record.alerted`). A new day rewrites the Kit save-the-date draft while Kit still reports it as `draft`; if it was already sent, a "New date" draft is made instead. A countdown that goes up on the reveal day itself sends a "Today is reveal day" reminder, not "Tomorrow". The admin page waits at most 4 s for the check. A failed JobTread read changes nothing.

**Rehearsals** (`state.test`): a job named `TEST <kid name> (<year>)` (`isTestJob` in `_reveal-invites.js`) runs the same path end to end. Its invites are sandboxed like the office job (test guests only, titled TEST). Its broadcast is scheduled with `test: true`, title `TEST · …`, and `visibleState()` turns it into idle for every public reader (`publicState`/`legacyState` unless `includeTest`, the hub, `/live/` SSR, the `.ics` download, viewer messages, replay). Reminders follow the same Settings switches but go by email to the test guests only (`sendTestReminder`), no Kit drafts, never the Kit live email. A real confirmed reveal always takes the slot (`planBroadcast` prefers non-test), and `tStartLive` never reuses a rehearsal, so a real stream goes public as its own broadcast. End-to-end run on production Oct 18 2026 with job `22PgVfPXZp6N` (closed afterwards): every step reached the site within about 5 seconds of the JobTread edit and nothing became public.

`source: 'manual'` is the emergency banner-only mode (old "Go Live Now"): no Stream input, links to Facebook, ends on its own timer. Stream state never touches a manual banner.

## Where things live

Data (D1 `soard-db`)

- `site_config` keys `broadcast` (state), `broadcast-config` (settings, live input record incl. ingest secrets, outputs, VAPID keys), `broadcast-health` (checks, webhook stats, deploy cooldown). All three are in `RUNTIME_ONLY_KEYS` in `scripts/d1-helpers.js`, so the prebuild never writes them to `src/content`.
- Tables (created on first use by `ensureSchema`): `broadcasts`, `broadcast_reminders`, `broadcast_sends` (idempotency per broadcast+kind+channel), `broadcast_claims` (one-time side effects per broadcast: the Facebook live post and its ids, the "Live now" and "Ended" alerts, the replay's Kit draft and rebuild), `broadcast_locks` (the tick lease), `broadcast_events` (analytics), `broadcast_messages` (messages for the family; IPs stored only as a hash salted with the broadcast id).
- Concurrency: ticks come from the hub, webhooks, visitors and the admin on any number of isolates. One tick (or admin command) runs at a time under a D1 lease (`broadcast_locks`): 15 s, extended before each step of a long tick, so a tick cut off mid-way blocks the next for at most two heartbeats, and a tick that finds its lease taken stops (`lease-lost`). An unforced tick also waits until no tick has started anywhere for its interval (20 s for visitors), public routes never force one, and a visitor that finds the lease busy never holds up the hub's heartbeat. Every state write bumps `state.rev`; a tick saves a phase change, before its side effects, only over the copy it read, and its final save merges over anything an admin saved meanwhile (`mergeState`, the admin wins ties). What going live and ending set off (`goLiveEffects`, `endedEffects`: the row, the Facebook post and its end, the "Live now" and "Ended" alerts, the "we're live" reminders through `dueReminderKinds`) runs on the tick that saves the change and on every tick for 10 minutes after, each until its claim exists, so a tick cut off right after saving loses none of it. `/api/broadcast-hooks` answers the hub within 10 s (it gives up at 15); a slower tick finishes after the reply and publishes its own change. The webhook's tick runs after its reply. Banner clicks are counted in place (`countBannerClick`), and `health.webhook` is written only by the webhook (`noteWebhook`), so neither races a tick.

Bindings and env

- `wrangler.toml` binds `LIVE_HUB` to `LiveHub@soard-live-hub`. The binding takes effect on the next Pages deploy after the Worker exists.
- Required: `CF_ACCOUNT_ID`, `CF_STREAM_TOKEN` (Stream: Edit), `RESEND_API_KEY`, `CF_PAGES_DEPLOY_HOOK`. Already set.
- Facebook: `FB_PAGE_ID`, `FB_PAGE_TOKEN` (already set; used for permalinks and API mode).
- Optional: `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_API_KEY_SID` + `TWILIO_API_KEY_SECRET` (a restricted key with only Messaging › Messages: Create; `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN` also works), `TWILIO_MESSAGING_SERVICE_SID` or `TWILIO_FROM` (staff alert texts); `SLACK_BOT_TOKEN` (the site's Slack app; pick the channel in Settings → Alerts) or the older `SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL`, `ALERT_EMAILS` (alerts); `KIT_API_KEY` (drafts, already set); `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` (lets the control room and alerts confirm YouTube is live: Google Cloud → APIs & Services → enable YouTube Data API v3 → Credentials → API key, restricted to that API).
- `STREAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (a secret): the same value as the secret on the Cloudflare Notifications webhook destination, which Cloudflare sends in the `cf-webhook-auth` header. With it set, a call without the right header is refused (401); a signed call forces an immediate tick (at most one every 5 s) and an input error alerts staff (once per input per 15 minutes, in our own words: nothing from the payload is copied). Without it, any call is only a hint: one ordinary throttled tick, no alerts. Either way the reply is `{ ok: true }`, and only Stream's three event names are stored or passed to the hub.
- Hub ticks are authenticated with one-time nonces (UUIDs) verified over the private binding; every decision is re-derived from Stream's lifecycle endpoint.

## Setup, once

1. Deploy the hub Worker (already deployed as `soard-live-hub` on the account; redeploy after edits with wrangler). Push the site so the `LIVE_HUB` binding applies.
2. Admin → Broadcast → Settings → Phones → **Create the live input**.
3. Scan the **Standard** QR with each phone that will stream (Larix Broadcaster installed). Scan **Weak signal** too; it adds a second profile.
4. Facebook: Live Producer → Streaming software → Advanced → Persistent stream key → paste into Settings → Facebook → Persistent stream key. Before each reveal, schedule the live post in Live Producer (a two-minute office task, any day before); Facebook auto-starts it when the stream arrives.
5. YouTube: Studio → Go live → Stream key → paste into Settings → YouTube → Stream key. Turn on Auto-start and Auto-stop in Studio.
5b. Instagram (optional, per reveal): Instagram issues a new Stream URL + key for every broadcast and has no API, so before each reveal someone on a desktop opens instagram.com → Create → Live, copies both, and pastes them into Settings → Instagram → Instagram Live too. Needs a professional account. Instagram Live is portrait; set Settings → Site → Camera orientation to what the crew shoots.
6. Cloudflare dashboard → Notifications → Destinations → Webhooks → Create with URL `https://sunshineonaranneyday.com/api/stream-webhook` and a Secret (a long random value). Add the same value in Cloudflare Pages → Settings → Variables and Secrets as the secret `STREAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and deploy. Set the destination's secret first: once the Pages secret is set, calls without it are refused. Then Notifications → Add → Stream → Stream Live Notifications → attach the webhook. (Optional but makes reactions instant, and it is how input errors reach staff. The Alerting API can do the same: `POST /accounts/{id}/alerting/v3/destinations/webhooks` with `secret`, then `POST /alerting/v3/policies` with `alert_type: stream_live_notifications`.)
7. Reminders → Generate push keys (browser alerts). Alerts → add alert emails, and staff mobile numbers for alert texts (needs the Twilio env vars; each new number gets a hello text).
8. Run checks in Health. Everything should read OK except optional rows.

## Control room and feed checks

The control room sits on Admin → Broadcast → **Now** while a reveal is live or about to start (deep link `/admin/#broadcast/control-room`, which every Slack post uses, opens Now). It plays the feed with its own HLS player instead of Stream's iframe, so the page can hear it:

- **Issues**: everything wrong right now, worst first, each with the fix, how long it has lasted, and its Slack status (posted at, reminders, or a failed post). New issues chime, put "⚠ <issue>" in the tab title, and can raise a desktop notification (toggles are remembered per browser). "Slack activity" lists every post for the reveal.
- **Sound**: an AudioWorklet (`public/admin/meter-worklet.js`) sees every sample. Loudness in LUFS per ITU-R BS.1770 (momentary bar, short-term mark and number, gated integrated since the page opened), true peak (4x oversampled, dBTP), left/right level bars (dBFS RMS with a sample-peak marker), and a minute of history. Verdicts: good, very quiet (short-term under -33 LUFS for 10 s), very loud (over -9 LUFS for 5 s), distorting (sample peak at -0.1 dBFS or true peak over 0 dBTP), one side only (20 dB apart for 8 s), no sound (6 s), no audio track. Speech should sit between -20 and -12 LUFS short-term. A mono mic reads on both meters. "Listen here" plays it out loud. The browser may ask for one click before it can listen.
- **Picture and signal**: feed size and frame rate, delay behind the phone, stalls, black or frozen picture, and a Signal chip (strong / unsteady / weak) from how fast video arrives versus real time and how often the phone has reconnected.
- **Destinations**: Website, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram Live, each OK / starting / needs a fix, with the fix and a button (Live Producer, YouTube Studio). Facebook in persistent-key mode can only see whether the Page has a live video; API mode adds Facebook's own ingest numbers, including audio bitrate. YouTube needs `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`. Instagram can't be checked.
- **Mic check**: the same player and meter on the private rehearsal input, from **Start mic check** on Now (or the go/no-go's mic check item). Watching it there (or the hub noticing it streaming) records the mic check the go/no-go looks for.
- **Go / no-go** (on Now while a reveal is scheduled): Stream input, mic check in the last 2 days, Facebook output, token and scheduled post, YouTube output and channel, Slack, the hub, the kid. Each open item says what to fix and has a button that goes there (Settings section, mic check, the JobTread job, Live Producer). It runs on its own an hour before a scheduled reveal and posts to Slack, and again 10 minutes before if anything was still open (`_broadcast-preflight.js`).

**Slack, with nobody watching.** While the phone is live or reconnecting, `runTick` checks the feed every 15 s (`_broadcast-monitor.js` → `watch`). Every problem is an issue with its own Slack post (and the alert emails): the problem, the fix, which reveal and how long it has been live, and a link to the control room. A reminder follows if it lasts 3 minutes, then every 10, and a "resolved" post when it clears. A minute into a healthy reveal (good sound, moving picture, Facebook and YouTube confirmed) it posts "All green" once. The staff numbers in Settings → Alerts are texted for the first post and the resolved one (not the reminders; a problem that comes back within 10 minutes of its last text doesn't text again, and texts one "Still:" if it then lasts 3 minutes), for "Live now", "All green", the go/no-go, a live Stream input error, a missed reveal and the reveal-day health check. Each text opens with a picture and what it is (🚨 serious or ⚠️ lesser "SOARD ALERT:", ✅ "SOARD FIXED:", 🟢 all green / 📡 live / ⏰ missed "SOARD:"), then the fix and a link, at most 306 characters (the emoji makes it Unicode, so 3 to 5 segments). The hello text stays plain. With `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN` set, Twilio reports delivery to `/api/twilio-status` (signed, public in `_middleware.js`): Settings → Alerts shows each number's last report, and a text that didn't arrive tells staff in Slack and by email once a day per number. Texts link to `/control-room/` (functions/control-room), not the admin: link previewers get a card (`/og/control-room.png`), people a 302 into the control room behind the sign-in. The hello text carries a contact card (`/alert-contact`, functions/alert-contact.js, a vCard with TWILIO_FROM and the app icon) so phones show "SOARD Reveal Alerts" and, as saved contacts, link previews. Site → Emails & invites → Reveal-day texts / Problem texts show every text as a phone would, and Text it to me sends them to a number on the alert list (each starting [Preview]). With the Slack app, each problem is one thread (`slackIssue` / `slackResolved` in `_broadcast-notify.js`, keyed `live:<broadcastId>:<issue>` in D1 `slack_open_posts`): reminders reply under the first post (also shown in the channel), and when it clears the first post is edited into "✅ Resolved: …". Every post is a card from `_slack-cards.js` (icon headline, lead, facts, what to do, buttons, footer; the phone notification is the headline and first line, never a URL). Posts have a kind: `news` (Live now, Ended, Reel posted, countdown up or moved) goes to the Good news channel, everything else to Needs attention (site_config "slack" `channel` and `attention`; without `attention` both go to `channel`). A failing problem while live (`urgent`: monitor errors, a live-input Stream error, a no-go) adds @here, but only when Needs attention has a channel of its own. Thresholds (`RULES`): no audio track after 20 s live; silent 30 s; very loud 20 s; one side 30 s; very quiet 2 min; frozen video 30 s; player can't load the feed 45 s; weak signal 30 s (video under 70% of real time, 2+ reconnects in 5 min, or Stream says reconnecting); phone disconnected 10 s; Facebook not live 2 min; YouTube not live 2.5 min. Slack delivery retries once on network errors, 429 and 5xx, and falls back to plain text if Slack rejects the formatting; a failed post shows in the Control room.

**When it ends**, the "Ended" Slack post carries a health summary (share of time the sound was good, average loudness, every issue with its time and length, when Facebook and YouTube went live, peak viewers, reconnects), and its row in History keeps it with a loudness line for the whole reveal (`broadcast_events` type `health_report`).

The server measures audio without a decoder: it Huffman-decodes the AAC spectrum of the newest segments for both channels, undoes mid/side and intensity stereo, and K-weights each line (`_aac-level.js`). Levels and LUFS match FFmpeg within 0.1 dB on noise, tones and stereo, about 1 dB on speech (TNS isn't undone). It has to, because Stream encodes at a constant bitrate and file sizes say nothing about sound.

## The live page

One page component serves `/live/` (follows whatever reveal is current) and `/live/<kid>/` (that kid only; another kid's reveal never shows there).

- **The family QR code** points at `/live/?src=qr` and never changes. Admin → Broadcast → Now has it as SVG and PNG. Scans count as `qr_scan` events in History.
- **`/live/` becomes the kid's page.** While a reveal is scheduled, live or on replay, `functions/live/[[path]].js` rewrites the static page for that kid before it leaves the server: headline, story, photo, page title and share preview (`/og/kids/<kid>.png`). The response is `no-store`. Texts and Slack fetch previews fresh; Facebook caches them, so Facebook posts should use the kid's own link (the system already does).
- **Unknown kid links** (kid still a draft, a typo, an old slug) redirect to `/live/` instead of "page not found". A reveal for a draft kid gets no countdown at all: Reveals → Now says the kid page needs publishing.
- **Frame timeline:** kid photo and countdown, then for the last 30 minutes before the start time the starting-soon screen (`/live/<kid>/slate/?embed=1&music=0`, same origin, silent; needs `'self'` in the CSP `frame-src`), then the live video when the phone connects, then the replay.
- **The video** is our own player (`src/utils/live-player.ts`), not Stream's iframe: hls.js (light build, loaded only when a video mounts) with LL-HLS first and plain HLS as the fallback, the browser's own HLS on iPhone (no hls.js download there), and the Stream iframe only if neither works. It needs the Stream customer host in the CSP `connect-src` and `blob:` in `media-src`. Owning the player is what lets the page start muted (with the `muted` attribute iPhone checks) and offer **Tap for sound**, reshape the frame to the real picture (portrait is the default; a phone held sideways still fits), and be honest about the feed: "The camera is getting ready" before the first frame, "Reconnecting" when the signal drops, with the kid's photo behind the note and the bar saying so instead of LIVE. It retries every few seconds on its own and picks back up when the phone reconnects; a frozen picture gets a fresh start after 12 s, and Safari's own HLS gets one if no frame shows within 12 s.
- **Simple, and nothing on the picture** (Peter's calls: the page is for people who have never been here, and nothing covers the reveal). During the watch party (starting soon, live, replay) the frame is a 9:16 video as tall as the screen allows with one bar under it: LIVE and the viewer count, then a single "Tap for sound"; once the sound is on, three controls: sound, one heart (others' love makes it beat; no numbers, nothing floats over the video) and full screen. ## Watch party and messages

On a live page during the starting-soon window and while live:

- **Love** (one heart on the page). Taps go over the WebSocket as `{"t":"react","k":"heart"}`; the hub rate limits each socket (15 per 3 s), batches every 400 ms and sends counts to everyone watching, whose heart button beats when love comes in (nothing floats over the video, no counts on the page). The page sends only `heart`; the hub still accepts the other kinds. Totals per reveal show in the admin.
- **Viewer count.** Live pages send `{"t":"watch"}`; only those sockets count (every page on the site holds a socket for the LIVE pill). Shown in the bar under the video from 5 viewers up ("N watching", or "N waiting" during the starting-soon window); it counts the site only, not Facebook or YouTube.
- **Messages for the family.** First name plus up to 280 characters. Every message is screened before anyone sees it: local rules (links, emails, phone numbers, social handles, a short word list), then Workers AI (`@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast`) with the context of a child's reveal, approving only kind, supportive or neutral notes. If screening is unavailable the message is **held** (fails closed) until someone approves it. Limits: 3 per visitor per minute, 20 per visitor per reveal, 30 per IP per minute (generous so a whole family on the house wifi can post), no duplicates, honeypot field. Shown messages reach everyone through the hub (`POST /relay` over the binding); without a hub that relays, pages poll every 20 s.
- **Admin:** Broadcast → Now → Messages: on/off switch (`config.messages.enabled`), waiting-for-review box, Hide/Show on every message, blocked ones folded away, and **Keepsake**, a print-ready page of every shown message to give the family. History links each past reveal's messages and keepsake.
- Messages stay open through the replay and close when the reveal clears.

**Deploy order:** the hub Worker carries the watch party (`watch`/`unwatch`, reactions, `/relay` for messages). Deploy it (`cd workers/live-hub && npx wrangler deploy`) before or with the site. With an older hub, messages still work (pages fall back to polling) but reactions and the viewer count don't, and people who leave the live page within the site keep counting as viewers. The site and hub work in either order for the October 2026 limits too: an older hub ignores `X-Soard-Client-Ip` (no per-address cap yet) and still serves `/ws` and `/status` on its workers.dev URL until it is redeployed with `workers_dev = false`; a newer hub with an older site skips the per-address cap when the header is missing.

## Instagram Reels (automatic)

Instagram has no API for going live, so every replay becomes a Reel instead (functions/api/_broadcast-reel.js), with no human step:

1. Replay ready → `state.reel` starts (`pending`). Recordings of 14:55 or less post whole (`fits`).
2. Longer ones: wait for Stream's English captions (requested when the replay is ready), score cues for reveal language ("open your eyes", countdowns, "your new room", reactions), take the densest 45 s window, and ask Workers AI (`env.AI`) for a second opinion. Agreement within 2 min → keyword time. Weak keywords → AI. Nothing found or no captions after 25 min → the last 14:55 (`fallback`). A per-reveal "reveal happens at minute" in the admin overrides everything.
3. Window = moment minus `leadSeconds` (default 4 min), up to 14:55, clamped to the recording. Stream `POST /stream/clip` makes a new video; the source is untouched.
4. Stream MP4 download → Instagram `POST /{ig-user-id}/media` (REELS, caption template, cover at the reveal) → poll `status_code` → `media_publish` → permalink.
5. The Now tab shows each stage, the chosen window and method, a preview link, and Retry / re-cut at a minute / Don't post. Outcomes alert; History links the Reel.

Needs the page token to carry `instagram_basic` and `instagram_content_publish` and a professional Instagram account linked to the Page. See docs/facebook-app-review.md → Instagram Reels. Without them the Reel stops at `blocked` with the reason and an alert; nothing else is affected.

The team shoots vertical, so Site behavior → Camera orientation defaults to portrait and the site's player is 9:16 until the picture arrives; after that it takes the picture's real shape.

## Facebook API mode

After Facebook approves the Live Video API for the app (see `docs/facebook-app-review.md`), switch Settings → Facebook to "Automatic (Live Video API)". On connect the site creates the live post (`status: LIVE_NOW`, title from the schedule), points a Stream output at the ingest URL Facebook returns, and ends the post on disconnect. If Facebook refuses (error #10/#200) the site records `fb.needsReview` and falls back to the persistent key without interrupting anything.

## Troubleshooting

- **Site slow to flip (10+ s)**: the Cloudflare notification is probably not set up; Health shows "never received". The hub still polls Stream every 8 s while armed/live.
- **Hub offline / LIVE_HUB missing**: pages fall back to polling `/api/broadcast` (10 s while live). Redeploy the Worker, then the site.
- **How a Facebook replay finds its kid page** (`functions/api/_fb-archive.js`, fully automatic, no scheduling needed): first rule with exactly one answer wins. (1) A reveal on the Broadcast page for that time, if one happens to exist. (2) A kid named in the post's first line, among this year's and last year's kids who have no reveal replay yet. (3) The same, searching the whole post. (4) A kid named in the first line from any year, if only one matches. Names match with `&` = `and`, in any order. Old kids thanked in a post (sponsors, past families) never qualify for rules 2 and 3. Automatic placements made under older rules are re-checked once and moved if they were wrong. Only a truly ambiguous post (two of this year's kids named) needs a human: the replay is saved, admins get one alert, and History → Facebook-only broadcasts has a kid picker.
- **Linking any Stream video by hand**: every Stream video box in the kid, event and community editors has "Choose from Stream", which browses the whole Stream library by name and shows which pages already use each video. No file on the computer is needed.
- **Two recordings for one reveal**: the phone was gone longer than the 30 s input timeout. Pick the right one in Now → Replay recording.
- **Encoder error alert (`ERR_GOP_OUT_OF_RANGE` etc.)**: re-scan the QR; it sets H.264, AAC, keyframe 2 s.
- **Audio problems**: use a wireless mic with its own receiver plugged into the phone, not Bluetooth (hands-free profile audio is low quality and drops in crowded rooms). Check Larix's level meter before starting, and do a mic check (Broadcast → Now → Start mic check): speech should sit between -24 and -12 dB on both meters.
- **Reset everything**: `POST /api/broadcast {action:'reset'}` from the admin console, or the Now tab if a broken state is showing.
