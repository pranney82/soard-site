Meet Leland — our little miracle

Leland was born with myelomeningocele, the most severe form of spina bifida. With the generous support of the Myfifident Foundation, SOARD provided Leland with a wheelchair-accessible bathroom and special toilet room.
   

A beautiful, medically functional space that supports Leland’s independence without feeling clinical.
